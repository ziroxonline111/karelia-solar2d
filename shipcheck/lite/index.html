Ship Check

Checks a Solar2D build against Google Play and App Store rules before you upload. Runs in this page only; your file never leaves the computer.

Drop an .apk, .aab or .ipa here or click to choose a file
This is Ship Check Lite. It checks native libraries (16 KB pages, 64-bit) and the Solar2D version. Ship Check Pro adds the rest: target API, Play Billing, Play Games v1, Google Sign-In, ads SDK and consent, debuggable builds, the R8 rule, and every App Store check including the usage-string rejections and a generated privacy manifest.
