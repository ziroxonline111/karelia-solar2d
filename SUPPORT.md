# Support for Karelia Labs Solar2D products

Karelia Consent, Karelia Web Kit and Karelia Ship Check (Lite and Pro).

## How to reach us

- **Email:** support@karelialabs.atlassian.net
- **Help desk:** https://karelialabs.atlassian.net/servicedesk/customer/portal/1 (enter your email; no account needed beforehand)
- **Issues on this repository:** for documentation errors and Ship Check Lite.

We answer within 14 days at the latest, as the marketplace vendor agreement requires, and fix confirmed bugs in a free update.

## What to include

| Product | Please send |
|---|---|
| Karelia Consent | `print( consent.debugReport() )` output, platform and Solar2D build number |
| Karelia Web Kit | `webkit.debugReport()` output, the portal, and the browser console log |
| Karelia Ship Check | the text from **Copy report** (it contains no part of your build) |
